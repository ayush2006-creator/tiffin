## Brand & Style

The design system bridges the comforting, artisanal warmth of traditional Indian dabbas (tiffin carriers) with the speed, clarity, and reliability of modern app design. It evokes the sensory delight of aromatic tempering (tadka/kadugu), comforting steam rising from freshly packed steel containers, and maternal reassurance.

### Target Audience & Tone
- **Audience:** Working professionals, students living away from home, and busy families seeking nutritious, homestyle regional Indian lunches and dinners without daily ordering friction.
- **Personality:** Warm, hospitable, wholesome, dependable, and appetizingly fresh.
- **Aesthetic Movement:** Tactile Modern Warmth with Organic Soft-Minimalism. It balances tactile culinary warmth (clay, turmeric, toasted spices) with crisp micro-interactions, clean negative space, and unambiguous operational states.

### Visual Principles
1. **Appetizing Clarity:** Photography and food callouts take center stage, framed by creamy warm neutrals rather than sterile stark whites.
2. **Culinary Cultural Signifiers:** Veg/Non-Veg indicators adhere strictly to standardized dietary codes while integrating seamlessly into modern card architecture.
3. **Frictionless Routine:** Weekly subscription schedulers, meal-swapping drawers, and delivery cadence toggles use friendly tactile affordances that make meal management feel effortless.

## Colors

The color architecture is built from natural spice blends, earthen cookware, and culinary staples.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#EA580C` - Terracotta Saffron):** Drives focal actions, active calendar selections, primary checkout/subscribe CTA banners, and non-veg dietary badges.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` - Turmeric Amber):** Highlights promotional callouts (such as the ₹1 trial offer), star ratings, warmth badges, and meal delivery status alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#16A34A` - Curry Leaf / Veg Green):** Dedicated identifier for pure vegetarian dishes, organic certifications, and active subscription verification states.
- **Neutral Light Canvas (`#FAF8F5` - Basmati Cream):** The underlying canvas, preventing stark cold eye-fatigue and offering an inviting, home-kitchen warmth.
- **Neutral Surface Elevated (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Reserved for card containers, bottom sheets, and interactive inputs to establish clear content layering over the cream background.
- **Typography Charcoal (`#1F2937`):** High-legibility neutral dark ensuring AAA accessibility against cream and white surfaces.
- **Muted Stone (`#6B7280`):** Secondary meta-information, ingredient descriptions, and inactive states.
- **Border Sand (`#EADFD5`):** Subtle boundaries that separate meal trays without harsh contrast.

## Typography

The design system employs **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies. Its rounded apertures, humanist shapes, and sturdy modern vertical metrics strike the ideal balance: soft enough to feel like personal home kitchen hospitality, yet geometric enough to convey precision, punctuality, and logistical trust.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Editorial Meal Headings:** Use `headline-lg` and `headline-xl` to introduce weekly themes, regional specials (e.g., "Kongunadu Lunch Box", "Chettinad Comfort Tiffin"), and onboarding hooks.
- **Nutritional & Ingredient Copy:** Keep `body-sm` and `body-md` in neutral dark `#1F2937` or muted stone `#6B7280` to preserve reading flow alongside complex thali component lists.
- **Dietary & Pricing Badges:** All pricing chips and Veg/Non-Veg metadata use `label-sm` or `label-md` with slight positive tracking (`0.02em` - `0.03em`) to guarantee quick recognition on smaller handheld screens.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid grid anchors the system, engineered around quick vertical scrolling, swipeable horizontal tiffin menus, and thumb-friendly meal customization.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile Handheld (360px – 599px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` outer margin, `1rem` gutters. Meal cards, calendar days, and delivery schedules stretch full-width or peek off-screen in horizontal carousels.
- **Tablet (600px – 1023px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` outer margin, `1rem` gutters. Meal discovery splits into a 2-column card grid with sticky weekly navigation.
- **Desktop / Large Dashboard (1024px+):** 12-column fixed-max system (`1200px`), `2.5rem` margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Left rail hosts subscription controls and calendar management, while center-right showcases daily tiffin configurations.

### Spatial Rhythm
- Compact internal paddings (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) are strictly used for badges, dietary chips, and meal tags.
- Structural card separation relies on `space-base` (16px) to maintain a scannable meal cadence without visual clutter.
- Canvas sections (e.g., "This Week's Thali Rotations" transitioning to "Subscription Plans") are separated by `space-xl` (32px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse ambient shadows coupled with stacked surface tones to replicate the physical warmth of layered tiffin carriers (dabbas).

### Surface Layers
- **Base Canvas:** The warm off-white `#FAF8F5` provides ambient foundation.
- **Surface Level 1 (Resting Cards & Modules):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-soft tinted shadow (`0 2px 8px -2px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`). This creates soft separation without aggressive borders.
- **Surface Level 2 (Selected Meal Tiles & Sticky Drawers):** Elevated via `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.12)` with a 1px border stroke (`#EADFD5`).
- **Surface Level 3 (Trial Overlays & Modal Bottom Sheets):** Modal drawers use `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.18)` backdrop-blended with a warm dimming scrim (`rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.45)`).

### Elevation Restraint
Avoid heavy dark drop shadows. All shadows must borrow a subtle warm-tinted alpha from the primary terracotta or charcoal neutral to reinforce an organic kitchen environment.

## Shapes

The design system utilizes roundedness level `2` (`0.5rem` base, `1rem` for large elements, and `1.5rem` for extra-large elements) to mirror the soft curved contours of traditional brass, copper, and stainless steel dabbas.

### Shape Geometry Rules
- **Buttons & Pill Filters:** Default to fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) or `rounded-lg` (`1rem`) to invite physical touch.
- **Meal Cards & Dabba Trays:** Styled with `rounded-lg` (`1rem`) to keep the interface approachable, friendly, and organic.
- **Badges & Food Indicators:** Small dietary tag badges (Veg / Non-Veg square frames) keep compact `0.25rem` corners to conform to official regulatory recognition standards, while promotional chips remain pill-shaped.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Button (Subscribe / Trial CTA):** Saffron Terracotta (`#EA580C`) background with crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 700`, `height: 48px`, `border-radius: 9999px` (pill). Active state features a warm glow shadow.
- **Secondary Action (Swap Meal / Customize Dabba):** White background with 1.5px border in `#EA580C`, text color `#EA580C`. Hover/tap transitions to `#FAF8F5`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button (Skip Day / Pause Plan):** Flat, no border, text color `#6B7280`, transitioning to `#1F2937` on focus.

### 2. Dietary Badges & Indicators
- **Standard Veg Indicator:** Square container (16x16px) with a 1.5px green outline (`#16A34A`), housing a centered solid circular dot (8px diameter) in `#16A34A`.
- **Standard Non-Veg Indicator:** Square container (16x16px) with a 1.5px terracotta outline (`#EA580C`), housing a centered solid equilateral triangle pointing upward in `#EA580C`.
- **Dietary Sub-Chips:** Soft pill pills (e.g., "No Onion No Garlic", "Millets Base", "Low Oil") using `#FAF8F5` background, `#6B7280` text, and subtle `#EADFD5` borders.

### 3. Meal Calendar & Day Picker Chips
- **Structure:** Vertical capsules displaying the abbreviated day (e.g., "MON"), date ("14"), and a micro-dot indicator indicating menu status (Lunch/Dinner selected).
- **Default State:** White background, `#6B7280` text, `0.5rem` roundedness.
- **Active State:** Solid `#EA580C` background, pure white text, elevated with a warm saffron shadow.
- **Paused / Skipped State:** Background `#E5E7EB`, text `#9CA3AF`, strikethrough date numeral.

### 4. Dabba Meal Cards
- **Structure:** Elevated white card containing an edge-to-edge curved top meal photograph (16:9 ratio), Veg/Non-Veg badge, regional kitchen origin tag ("Homestyle Andhra"), item breakdown (e.g., "3 Phulkas, Dal Tadka, Poriyal, Curd Rice"), and an action footer.
- **Interactive Footer:** Houses direct meal customizer toggle ("Swap to Millet Roti") and nutritional summary snippet (Calories, Protein).

### 5. Input Fields & Delivery Time Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Warm white `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px `#EADFD5` border, rounded `0.75rem`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Active focus shifts border to `#EA580C` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.15)` focus ring.
- **Radio & Checkbox (Meal Slots):** Radio buttons use `#EA580C` when selected. Tiffin lunch/dinner toggles use segmented switch bars with smooth slide transitions.

### 6. Transparent ₹1 Trial Banner & Subscription Bar
- **Trial Strip:** High-contrast amber card (`#F59E0B`) with `#1F2937` bold typography, showcasing an energetic trial tag ("Try your first homestyle dabba for ₹1") followed by a sticky primary action button to build trust and eliminate upfront subscription anxiety.
